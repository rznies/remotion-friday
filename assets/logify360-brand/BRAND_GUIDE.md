## Video Production Recommendations

### Color Palette for Video

- Use orange (#EF6F25) for CTAs, highlights, and accent animations
- Dark gray (#333333) or near-black for text on white backgrounds
- White (#FFFFFF) as primary background
- Light peach (#FEF5EF) for subtle highlights and backgrounds

### Typography in Video

- Use clean, modern sans-serif fonts (IBM Plex Sans or similar)
- Maintain high contrast for readability
- Bold headings, clear hierarchy

### Visual Elements

- Rounded corners (12px) on cards, buttons, and UI elements
- Subtle shadows for depth
- Clean, minimal design aesthetic
- Tech-forward but approachable feel

### Animation Style

- Smooth, professional transitions
- Medium energy pace
- Clear, direct messaging
- Focus on problem-solving and ease of use
